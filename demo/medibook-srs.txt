The system should respond quickly when patients search for available doctors.
The system shall support many concurrent users.
Appointment reminders shall be sent before the visit.
The app shall support popular payment methods such as credit cards, UPI, etc.
The receptionist shall review the doctor's schedule before she confirms it with the patient.
The system shall allow editing of appointments by patients with insurance.
If the patient is a minor or a senior and has a referral, the system shall waive the registration fee.
Patient records shall be retained according to the hospital policy.
The user interface shall be intuitive for elderly users.
The system shall release the slot when a booking is cancelled.
Duplicate bookings shall be rejected.
The system shall be available most of the time.
The system shall notify patients by email, SMS, and so on.
The system shall lock a user account after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
The system shall send a confirmation email to the patient within 60 seconds of a successful booking.
The system shall display the consultation fee in INR including 18% GST on the booking page.
The system shall allow a patient to cancel an appointment up to 2 hours before its start time.
The system shall require two-factor authentication for all administrator accounts.
The search page shall return results within 2 seconds for at least 95% of requests.
